The fine print

In plain words, before you ask.

01

Why does every plan cost the same?

Because we do not hold features back to sell you the tier above. The rate per user is the same whichever block you take. What you are choosing is how many seats you need, not how much of the product you are allowed to have.

02

What is a seat block?

A fixed number of seats, not a range — ten, twenty, fifty or a hundred and fifty. You take the block and everyone in it gets their own login across every app the block carries.

03

Is GST included in the price?

No. Everything shown is before tax. GST is added at the statutory rate and itemised in full on the checkout screen and on the invoice, so the number you approve is the number you pay.

04

How is it billed?

Annually — one invoice a year for the block, not a per-seat meter that creeps every time someone joins or a monthly line that quietly renews.

05

What if we outgrow the block?

Move up to the next one. Write to us and we will size it with you rather than have you discover the ceiling on a Monday morning.

06

Can we take our data out?

Yes. Your books, contacts, recordings and files sit in one place and export whole, whenever you want. No part of your business is held in a format only we can read.

07

Do we have to use everything?

No, and you do not pay less for ignoring an app. Everything the block carries is switched on from day one; the ones you do not open simply sit quiet until you want them.

08

Still not sure which block?

Count the people who will log in this year, then take the block above it. Talking to us costs nothing and we would rather size it right than sell you a block you grow out of in March.
