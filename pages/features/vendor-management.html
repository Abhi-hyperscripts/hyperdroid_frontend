Performance measured
from your own orders.

Not a survey and not an opinion. Vendor performance is computed in the database from the purchase orders you actually raised — what was delivered, when, and against what was promised.

Scorecards fail because they are filled in. This one is derived, so it is current whether or not anybody remembered.
