The business OS · People

Every payslip keeps
its arithmetic.

The days counted, the proration applied and the rule behind each statutory deduction stay attached to the payslip that used them — so the answer to "why is this figure this figure" is on the document rather than in someone’s memory.

Country rules are data, not code. A payroll engine that hard-codes provident fund can only ever run one country, and every rate change becomes a release.

Payroll

A run you can take apart.

The workings surviveEach payslip carries how it was computed, not just what it concluded.
Checks before it passesThe engine asserts a named set of checks before it will let a run through, so an obviously wrong payroll does not reach the bank file.
Statutory from configurationDeductions come from a country pack rather than from code, which is why the same engine runs India, the UAE, the UK and the US.
Loans through the payslipAn advance or loan is recovered as an instalment on the payslip, with the balance tracked rather than remembered.
Time

Attendance and leave.

Attendance with geofencingClock in and out against an office with a location, for the roles where being there is the point.
RegularisationA missed punch is corrected through a request with an approval, not by an administrator editing the record.
Leave with approvalsRequests that route to the manager who should decide them, with the balance computed rather than tracked in a sheet.

Departments and officesA hierarchy the whole platform uses, so a project’s team and a payroll’s department are the same records.
Managers auto-assignedHaving direct reports is what makes someone a manager, rather than a separate flag somebody forgets to set.
