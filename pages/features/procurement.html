The spreadsheet problem

Most teams run procurement on email and Excel. It works—until it doesn't.

Without Ragenaizer
Email vendors one by one, then chase them for replies. Quotes arrive as PDFs, WhatsApp messages, phone calls.
Copy-paste into Excel to compare. Hope you didn't miss a column. Always pick the cheapest—and regret it when they deliver late.
No memory. When procurement person leaves, all vendor knowledge walks out the door. Next person starts from scratch.
No accountability. Can't prove why you picked Vendor A over Vendor B. No audit trail when things go wrong.
With Ragenaizer
Send one link. Vendors submit structured quotes in a portal. No chasing, no lost attachments, no phone tag.
AI compares instantly on price, quality, and delivery. Recommends the best overall vendor—not just the cheapest.
Institutional memory. Every quote, every decision, every vendor rating stays in the system. Knowledge compounds, never leaves.
Full audit trail. Every decision is recorded with reasons. Prove compliance, justify spend, and hold vendors accountable.
