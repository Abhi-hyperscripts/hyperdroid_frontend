The business OS · Files

A link you can
take back.

Files up to 5 GB, uploaded in chunks so a dropped connection does not mean starting again, and shared with links that carry a password, an expiry and a download limit — because most business sharing should not be permanent.

Sharing is where storage products get careless. A link that lives forever and works for anyone is the one that leaks.

Getting files in

Up to 5 GB, over a connection that drops.

5 GB per fileLarge enough for the video, the design file and the database dump people actually need to send.
Chunked uploadA large file goes up in pieces and resumes, rather than failing at ninety percent on a bad connection.
Direct or presignedSmaller files upload straight through; larger ones use a presigned path so the transfer does not queue behind the application.
Sharing

A link with conditions attached.

Password protectedLinks can require a password, hashed rather than stored, so a forwarded URL is not enough on its own.
ExpiryA link that stops working on a date, which is what most business sharing actually wants.
Download limitsCapped at a number of downloads, so a link shared with one person cannot quietly serve fifty.
Knowing what happened

An audit log, not a vibe.

Who did whatUploads, downloads, deletions and share creation recorded with the user and the time.
Live updatesChanges appear for everyone with access as they happen, rather than on refresh.
