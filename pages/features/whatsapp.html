CRM · WhatsApp

The AI drafts it.
A person sends it.

WhatsApp conversations attached to the lead, with templates, bulk sending, and AI-suggested replies that sit in a pending queue until somebody approves them. The AI can be paused on a conversation the moment it should not be answering.

Approval is the point. An AI that replies to customers unsupervised is a liability; one that drafts and waits is leverage.
