Every feature you need

Powerful video conferencing without the complexity or hidden fees

HD Video & Audio

Crystal-clear video that automatically adjusts to your connection. Built on LiveKit WebRTC for enterprise-grade reliability.

  • Adaptive quality for any bandwidth
  • Echo cancellation & noise suppression
  • Active speaker detection

Screen Sharing

Share your entire screen, a specific window, or a browser tab. Viewers can zoom and pan for details.

  • Full screen or window sharing
  • Viewer zoom & pan controls
  • Screenshot capture

Recording

Record from your browser when you need a copy. The file is written on your own machine when you stop — we never hold it.

  • Records in your browser
  • Saved to your own machine
  • Nothing stored on our servers

Host Controls

Control your meetings with a waiting room, participant management, and access controls.

  • Waiting room / lobby
  • Admit / reject participants
  • Participant whitelist

In-Meeting Chat

Share links, notes, and files during meetings. Chat history persists so you can review later.

  • Real-time messaging
  • File sharing in chat
  • Persistent history

Live Captions

Speech to text as people speak, produced per speaker on the server rather than in one participant’s browser. A transcript and a summary follow the call.

  • Captions during the meeting
  • Separated by speaker
  • Transcript and summary after
