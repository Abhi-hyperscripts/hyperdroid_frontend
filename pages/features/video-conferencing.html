The business OS · Meetings

The meeting, and
everything it produced.

HD video and screen sharing, live captions generated while people are talking, and a recording that lands in your own Drive when it ends — not on a vendor’s servers under a retention policy you did not write.

One module, on the same login. The people in the meeting are the people in your HRMS, with the permissions they already have.

In the room

Video, screens and the things people actually do.

HD videoReal-time media over WebRTC, with active-speaker detection so the grid follows the conversation.
Screen sharing with zoomShare a screen and let viewers zoom and pan it — because the thing being demonstrated is usually smaller than the screen it is on.
Chat, reactions and hand raiseThe side channel a meeting needs, kept with the meeting rather than in a different app.
Picture in pictureKeep the call visible while working in another window.
Live captions

Produced while the meeting happens.

A transcription bot joins the meeting, subscribes to each participant’s audio, and streams the text back to everyone.

Per participantEach speaker is transcribed on their own stream, so the captions can attribute what was said.
Server-sideThe work happens on the server rather than in each browser, so a laptop under load does not lose the captions.
Afterwards

Recordings that are yours.

Recorded to your DriveA finished recording is uploaded into your own Drive, under your own storage and your own retention.
Lobbies and hosted meetingsA hosted meeting waits for its host; a participant-controlled one admits only the people on its list.
