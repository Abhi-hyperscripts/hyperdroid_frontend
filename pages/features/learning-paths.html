Learning · Programmes

Joining the role
assigns the training.

A path is several courses in order. Training rules assign the right path when somebody joins a department or a role — which is the difference between onboarding that happens and onboarding somebody has to remember to start.

Compliance training fails at assignment. Not at delivery — at nobody noticing the new joiner needed it.
