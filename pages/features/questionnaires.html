Research · Instruments

Parsed, and
correctable.

A questionnaire is uploaded and parsed into blocks you can actually inspect. When the parse is wrong — and on a real instrument it sometimes is — you correct it and re-parse, with a stage log and an audit of what changed.

Most tools parse silently. Which means the first sign of a bad parse is a cross-tab that makes no sense.

Parsing

Into blocks you can see.

Upload and parseThe instrument as it exists, rather than re-entered by hand.
Blocks and chunksThe parsed structure, inspectable, so a misread question is visible before it reaches analysis.
Correct and re-parseEdit the parsed output, or run the parse again — both are supported rather than requiring a re-upload.
The trail

Stage logs and audit.

Stage logsWhat the pipeline did at each step, which is where you look when the output is wrong.
AuditWhat a human changed, so a corrected parse is distinguishable from an original one.
ActivationA questionnaire becomes active deliberately, rather than the moment it is uploaded.
Validation

Before it reaches analysis.

Validation as a stepRun against the instrument and the data, so a mismatch is found before a cross-tab makes it look like a finding.
ChunksThe parsed pieces, addressable individually, which is what makes a targeted correction possible instead of a full re-upload.
