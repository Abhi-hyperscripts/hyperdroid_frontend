What is Recruitment?

Post a job, define the application form, share the public link on LinkedIn or anywhere. Candidates fill the form — no resume needed — and applications land here automatically.

  1. Click + New Posting, fill the job details, and customise the form fields (text, email, phone, dropdown, etc.).
  2. Copy the public apply link from the posting card and share it.
  3. Switch to the Applications tab to review and move applicants through the pipeline.
