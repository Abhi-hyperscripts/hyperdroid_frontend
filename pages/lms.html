Straight answers

The things people ask on the first call.

01

Do we have to enrol people manually?

No. Training rules enrol automatically — you define who should be doing what, and a rule can be executed to bring everyone matching it onto the right courses. New joiners stop depending on somebody remembering.

02

Can a certificate be checked by someone outside?

Yes. Certificates carry a number that can be verified directly, so an auditor or a client can confirm one without an account or a phone call to your HR team.

03

Is it only for employees?

No. The same courses work for customer education and partner training. Employees come from the HR records you already have; others do not have to exist there.

04

Can we run live training as well as recorded?

Yes. Live sessions run on the platform's own meeting infrastructure, so the class and its transcript end up in the same workspace instead of a separate conferencing account.

05

How do we know who has actually finished?

Progress is recorded as people move through lessons rather than inferred at the end, and reporting rolls it up by person, team and course.

06

Can courses be assessed properly?

Quizzes for knowledge and assignments for work that needs reviewing. Clicking to the last slide is not treated as evidence of anything.

07

Is Learning billed separately?

No — it is one of the apps in the seat block, sharing the same login and the same people records as HR.
