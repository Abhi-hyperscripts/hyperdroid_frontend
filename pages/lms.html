Features

Everything you need to train and certify

A learning platform built for the way modern teams actually train people — onboarding, ongoing skill development, customer education and compliance, all in one place.

Course Builder With Modules & Lessons

Structure courses the way you teach: modules, lessons, embedded video, documents and rich content. Publish, archive and duplicate courses with one click. Categories and a course catalog make discovery easy for learners.

Quizzes With Auto-Grading

Add quizzes to any lesson with multiple question types, set pass marks, and let the system auto-grade and track every attempt. Learners see their score immediately; you see the full attempt history per learner.

Assignments With File Submissions

Assign essays, projects and file uploads. Learners submit, instructors grade, and feedback flows back into the learner dashboard. Every submission is tracked, scored and exportable.

Live Training Sessions

Schedule live virtual classroom sessions powered by the platform's video conferencing. Track who attended, who joined late, who didn't show. The session recording goes straight into the course for anyone who missed it.

Auto-Enroll New Hires

Set training rules ("everyone in the engineering department must complete InfoSec 101 within 30 days of joining") and the system handles enrollment automatically. New hires get the right training the day they start. Compliance becomes a background job.

Verifiable Certificates

Issue certificates of completion with custom templates and a public verification link. Anyone — recruiters, regulators, customers — can verify a certificate is real with one click. No more PDF forgeries.

Learning Paths

Chain courses into a structured learning path — onboarding, sales enablement, manager training, certification programs. Enroll learners in the whole path with one click and the system tracks progress across every course in the sequence.

Bulk Enrollment for Teams & Cohorts

Enroll a whole department, a new hire batch or a customer cohort in seconds. Pick the people, pick the course, click enrol — everyone gets access at once and managers see the full roster.

Course Discussions

Built-in discussion forums on every course. Threaded replies, mark a reply as the solution, search the archive. Learners help learners and instructors don't have to answer the same question twice.

Progress & Compliance Tracking

See exactly where every learner is — completed lessons, quiz scores, course completion percentage, training compliance status. The compliance dashboard shows who's behind on mandatory training. Report exports for audits.

Manager Reports & Team Analytics

Managers get a team report showing every direct report's training status, course completions and certificates earned. Course-level analytics show which lessons learners struggle with so you can improve content where it matters.

Leaderboards & Reviews

Drive engagement with leaderboards based on courses completed and certificates earned. Let learners review and rate courses so the best content rises to the top.

Course Materials in Drive

Videos, documents, slides and assignment submissions live in Drive — secure, versioned, audit-logged. Reuse the same files across courses without uploading twice.

Course Discussions in Chat

Threaded course discussions, course-wide announcements and instructor DMs all flow through the same messaging layer your team already uses. No second app to teach.
