Ragenaizer Mobile App for Android

Clock in with a selfie, apply for leave, read payslips, chat with your team, join meetings and work your CRM leads — the whole business OS in one app. Sign in with the account your company gave you.

Download APK Release notes

Android 8.0 or newer · about 190 MB

Attendance that proves itself

Selfie clock-in inside the office geofence, signed with your fingerprint or face. Field agents share a live route with their manager only while clocked in.

Everything HR, on your phone

Leave requests and balances, payslips and salary breakdown, claims, loans and the team directory, all from the same HRMS your company runs on the web.

Work, not just admin

CRM leads with auto-logged calls, WhatsApp inbox, team chat, video meetings, Drive, projects and mail, sharing one login and one permission model.

How to install

  1. Tap Download APK above on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded ragenaizer-ess.apk from your notifications or Files app.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  4. Open Ragenaizer and sign in with your work email and password. Accounts are created by your company admin; there is no public sign-up.

Updates install over the existing app and keep your sign-in. The app is also in review on Google Play; this page will carry the Play Store link as soon as it is public. Questions: support@ragenaizer.com.
