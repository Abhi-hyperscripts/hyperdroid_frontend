Features

Share Securely

Send files to anyone with a link. Add password protection, set expiration dates, or limit downloads for sensitive documents.

Know Who Accessed What

Complete activity tracking for every file. See who viewed, downloaded, or shared something—perfect for compliance and peace of mind.

Your Storage, Your Choice

Works with AWS, Wasabi, or your own storage. Keep your files wherever makes sense for your organization.

Always Up to Date

Changes sync instantly across your team. When someone uploads a file or creates a folder, everyone sees it right away.

Three Upload Modes

Direct upload for files under 100MB. S3 multipart for files up to 5GB. Chunked upload with resume support for unreliable networks. The system picks the optimal mode automatically — you just drag and drop.

Presigned URLs

Generate time-limited download URLs for secure file access. No authentication required for the recipient — just a link that expires. Perfect for sharing with external partners and clients.

Meeting Recordings

Vision meeting recordings automatically save to Drive. Access, share and audit recorded meetings from one place. Password-protect recordings with expiry dates and download limits.

Resumable Uploads, Even on Bad Wi-Fi

Big file, flaky connection? Chunked uploads pick up exactly where they stopped — initiate, stream chunks, check status, complete. Pause, resume, even abort. The file gets there.

Star Files for Quick Access

Star the documents you actually use every day so they're one click away. The contracts you reference, the spreadsheets you open daily, the templates you re-use — all in your starred view.

Revoke or Re-Issue Any Share Link

Sent a link to the wrong person? Revoke it. Need to add a password after the fact? Update the existing share. Change the expiry, change the download cap, rotate the password — all without breaking the recipient's URL.

Per-Share Download Caps

Set a maximum number of downloads per share. Once it's hit, the link stops working. Combine with a password and an expiry for the safest way to send sensitive documents.

One Source for Every File

Drive isn't just for the files you upload. It's the single source of truth for meeting recordings from Vision, file attachments from Chat, audio recordings from Research focus groups, and documents from HR. One place to find anything.
