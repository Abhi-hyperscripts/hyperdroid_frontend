Being straight

When you should stay where you are.

A comparison page that finds no reason to keep the other tool is not a comparison, it is an advert. Three honest ones.

You only need this one thing

If Cornerstone is the only software your business runs and you have no intention of adding a CRM, projects or HR, a specialist tool will go deeper in its own lane than we will. Our argument is the stack, not the single app.

Your team is fluent in it

Retraining has a real cost, and it lands on the people who are busiest. If everyone is fast in Cornerstone and nothing is actually breaking, the licence saving may not cover the disruption this year.

You depend on something we lack

Check the table below against the handful of things you genuinely could not work without. If one of them is missing here, that is a reason to wait — ask us and we will tell you honestly whether it is on the way.

What leaves your stack

The things you stop running.

Not features you gain — work you stop doing. That is usually where the real saving is, and it never appears on a licence invoice.

Multi-Year Enterprise Contracts

Cornerstone is sold on multi-year contracts in the tens or hundreds of thousands. The product is built for HR teams with seven-figure L&D budgets, not for fast-moving SMBs.

Ragenaizer: monthly per-user, no minimums, no multi-year lock-in

Months-Long Implementation

Cornerstone rollouts take months. A dedicated implementation team, change management, integrations with HRIS and payroll, training the trainers. Your team is using spreadsheets in the meantime.

Ragenaizer: signup → first cohort enrolled the same day

Aging Enterprise UX

Cornerstone's interface looks and feels like the early-2010s enterprise SaaS it is. Learners notice. Engagement suffers compared to consumer-grade learning experiences.

Ragenaizer: modern UI built for the 2026 workforce

Talent-Only Silo

Cornerstone is a talent management platform. Sales, projects, accounting — separate tools, separate logins, separate licensing contracts. Five vendors for one team.

Ragenaizer: LMS plus the rest of the business platform
