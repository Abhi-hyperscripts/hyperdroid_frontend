Pharma depth, without the Windows box. Fifty-three capabilities across eight domains — including the three where Marg wins.

The short answer

Marg is genuinely strong where it matters for pharma distribution, and it earned that. It is also a Windows product with per-licence multi-user, and the rest of your company — sales, people, projects — sits outside it. Ragenaizer carries the same pharma depth (Schedule-H register, salts and substitutes, per-batch MRP, FEFO, racks) and puts it in a browser next to the CRM, the HR and the books.
