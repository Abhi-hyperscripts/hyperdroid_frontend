How these pages are written

  • Every Ragenaizer claim is read out of our own source code before it goes on a page. Where we counted something — endpoints, tools, invariants — the number is on the page so you can hold us to it.
  • Every competitor claim comes from their published documentation and is not verified by us. Prices especially: get a current quote from them before you decide anything.
  • Our gaps are stated. E-way bill, GSTR-2A/2B reconciliation and GSTR-9 are not built, and every relevant page says so in red rather than leaving the row out.
  • Every comparison carries three reasons to stay where you are. A page that finds no reason to keep the other tool is an advert, not a comparison.
