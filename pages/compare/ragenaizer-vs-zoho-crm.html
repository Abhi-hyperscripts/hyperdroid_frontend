Common Zoho Frustrations

Problems Ragenaizer solves out of the box

50 Apps, 50 UIs

Zoho One is famous for its breadth — and infamous for the fact that every product has a slightly different navigation, slightly different fields, slightly different bugs. New hires spend weeks just learning where things live.

Ragenaizer: one consistent UI for everything

Cross-App Sync Gaps

"Sync" between Zoho CRM and Zoho Books is real but lossy. Tags, custom fields and history don't always survive the journey. Customers ask "why is this different in Books?" and the answer is "because they're separate products."

Ragenaizer: one customer record, no sync needed

No Universal Search

Search in Zoho CRM finds CRM records. Search in Zoho Books finds Books records. There's no single search bar that finds anything matching "Acme Corp" across the whole platform.

Ragenaizer: one search bar, one set of results

Zia (the AI) Is Enterprise-Tier Only

Zoho's AI features — including AI lead scoring and AI prediction — are gated to Enterprise tier and above. The cheaper plans get a brochure version.

Ragenaizer: AI lead discovery on every plan
