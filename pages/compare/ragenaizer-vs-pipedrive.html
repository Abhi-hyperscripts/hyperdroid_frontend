Being straight

When you should stay where you are.

A comparison page that finds no reason to keep the other tool is not a comparison, it is an advert. Three honest ones.

You only need this one thing

If Pipedrive is the only software your business runs and you have no intention of adding a CRM, projects or HR, a specialist tool will go deeper in its own lane than we will. Our argument is the stack, not the single app.

Your team is fluent in it

Retraining has a real cost, and it lands on the people who are busiest. If everyone is fast in Pipedrive and nothing is actually breaking, the licence saving may not cover the disruption this year.

You depend on something we lack

Check the table below against the handful of things you genuinely could not work without. If one of them is missing here, that is a reason to wait — ask us and we will tell you honestly whether it is on the way.

What leaves your stack

The things you stop running.

Not features you gain — work you stop doing. That is usually where the real saving is, and it never appears on a licence invoice.

No Built-In Video Meetings

Want to call a prospect from inside the CRM? Pipedrive sends you to a third-party tool. Schedule a Zoom, copy the link, paste it into Pipedrive's notes. Friction every time.

Ragenaizer: one-click video call from any deal

Won Deal Doesn't Become a Customer

Pipedrive marks a deal as "won" — and that's it. Your finance team still has to create the customer record manually in QuickBooks/Xero. Two systems, two places to update, one drift problem.

Ragenaizer: won deal auto-creates the customer in Accounts

No Multi-Pipeline on Cheaper Plans

Sales pipeline plus partner pipeline plus renewals pipeline? On Pipedrive's Essential plan you get one. Multiple pipelines start at the Advanced tier — bumping the per-seat cost ~30%.

Ragenaizer: unlimited pipelines on every plan
