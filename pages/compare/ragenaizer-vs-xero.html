Common Xero Frustrations

Problems Ragenaizer Accounts solves out of the box

India Doesn't Exist for Xero

Xero shut down its India operation. If you need GSTR-1, GSTR-3B or TDS returns, Xero literally cannot help you — you need an entirely different vendor.

Ragenaizer: India GST built in, with one-click setup

Starter Plan Bill Caps

Xero's Starter plan caps you at 20 invoices and 5 bills per month. Cross either threshold and you're upgraded automatically. The "starter" plan is really a trial.

Ragenaizer: unlimited invoices and bills on every plan

Standalone Accounting

Xero only does books. Sales pipeline, projects, payroll, HR — you're buying separate tools and gluing them together with the Xero App Marketplace and a lot of patience.

Ragenaizer: CRM, projects, payroll all share one customer record

Per-User Add-Ons Stack Up

Xero Expenses, Xero Projects, Xero Payroll — each adds per-user fees on top of the base subscription. By the time you've enabled the modules you actually want, the bill has doubled.

Ragenaizer: every module included on every plan
