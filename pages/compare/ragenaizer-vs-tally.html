Common Tally Frustrations

Problems Ragenaizer Accounts solves out of the box

It's a Windows Desktop App

Tally Prime runs on Windows. Period. Mac users boot a VM. Linux users are out of luck. Your accountant works from home? Either VPN into the office machine or buy "Tally on Cloud" from a reseller.

Ragenaizer: works in any browser, any OS

Annual TSS Just to Stay Updated

Tally's TSS (Total Solutions Service) is an annual subscription you pay just to keep getting product and tax updates. Skip it and your software stops being compliant.

Ragenaizer: continuous updates included

Multi-User Means the License Tier Up

Tally Prime Single User to Multi User is a separate, more expensive license. Add a second accountant and the bill jumps. There's no flexible per-seat scaling.

Ragenaizer: pay per user, scale one at a time

No Real Integrations

Tally is a closed system. Sales, projects, payroll all live in separate tools and the data eventually gets typed back into Tally by hand at month-end. Two systems of truth, one cleanup job.

Ragenaizer: CRM, projects, payroll all flow into the books
