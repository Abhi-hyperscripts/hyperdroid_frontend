Being straight

When you should stay where you are.

A comparison page that finds no reason to keep the other tool is not a comparison, it is an advert. Three honest ones.

You only need this one thing

If Tally is the only software your business runs and you have no intention of adding a CRM, projects or HR, a specialist tool will go deeper in its own lane than we will. Our argument is the stack, not the single app.

Your team is fluent in it

Retraining has a real cost, and it lands on the people who are busiest. If everyone is fast in Tally and nothing is actually breaking, the licence saving may not cover the disruption this year.

You depend on something we lack

Check the table below against the handful of things you genuinely could not work without. If one of them is missing here, that is a reason to wait — ask us and we will tell you honestly whether it is on the way.

What leaves your stack

The things you stop running.

Not features you gain — work you stop doing. That is usually where the real saving is, and it never appears on a licence invoice.

Annual TSS Just to Stay Updated

Tally's TSS (Total Solutions Service) is an annual subscription you pay just to keep getting product and tax updates. Skip it and your software stops being compliant.

Ragenaizer: continuous updates included

Multi-User Means the License Tier Up

Tally Prime Single User to Multi User is a separate, more expensive license. Add a second accountant and the bill jumps. There's no flexible per-seat scaling.

Ragenaizer: pay per user, scale one at a time

No Real Integrations

Tally is a closed system. Sales, projects, payroll all live in separate tools and the data eventually gets typed back into Tally by hand at month-end. Two systems of truth, one cleanup job.

Ragenaizer: CRM, projects, payroll all flow into the books
