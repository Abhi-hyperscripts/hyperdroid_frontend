Common TalentLMS Frustrations

Problems Ragenaizer LMS solves out of the box

Live Sessions Need Zoom

TalentLMS doesn't host video. You bolt on a Zoom integration, manage two licenses and learners need to follow links between systems for live classes.

Ragenaizer: live sessions on the platform's own video

Disconnected From HR

TalentLMS doesn't know who your employees are. You either upload CSVs or build a sync with BambooHR / Workday and pray the cohorts stay in sync.

Ragenaizer: HRMS feeds learners automatically

Active-User Caps

TalentLMS pricing is based on "active users per month" with hard caps. Cross the cap and your account upgrades or new learners get blocked. Cohort training pushes you onto the next tier fast.

Ragenaizer: predictable per-user pricing, no surprise upgrades

Automation Locked at Higher Tiers

TalentLMS's "Automations" feature — the one that auto-enrolls learners based on rules — is gated to the higher-priced plans. The basic plan does manual enrollment only.

Ragenaizer: training rules engine on every plan
