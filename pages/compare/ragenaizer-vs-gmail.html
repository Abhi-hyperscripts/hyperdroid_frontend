Being straight

When you should stay where you are.

A comparison page that finds no reason to keep the other tool is not a comparison, it is an advert. Three honest ones.

You only need this one thing

If Gmail is the only software your business runs and you have no intention of adding a CRM, projects or HR, a specialist tool will go deeper in its own lane than we will. Our argument is the stack, not the single app.

Your team is fluent in it

Retraining has a real cost, and it lands on the people who are busiest. If everyone is fast in Gmail and nothing is actually breaking, the licence saving may not cover the disruption this year.

You depend on something we lack

Check the table below against the handful of things you genuinely could not work without. If one of them is missing here, that is a reason to wait — ask us and we will tell you honestly whether it is on the way.

What leaves your stack

The things you stop running.

Not features you gain — work you stop doing. That is usually where the real saving is, and it never appears on a licence invoice.

Paying for Docs and Meet you never open

Google Workspace bundles Gmail with Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, Calendar, Admin and more. Most teams use a fraction of that. You're buying the whole bundle to get email.

Pay only for the email experience, skip the bundle

sales@ needs its own seat

Want a shared inbox for sales@, support@ or info@ where the whole team can read and reply? Google charges you another full license per shared address. Costs quietly compound.

Unlimited shared mailboxes — no per-alias fee

Your mail lives on someone else's servers

For regulated industries — finance, healthcare, legal, government, anyone with data residency requirements — customer correspondence sitting on Google's US infrastructure is a compliance conversation you'd rather not have.

Your mailbox provider keeps the mail — we're just the UI

Vendor lock-in by design

Once you switch your MX records to Google, you can migrate off, but it's a project with downtime, DNS changes, mailbox exports and retraining. "Don't like our pricing? Good luck leaving."

Change mail providers anytime — your setup doesn't care
