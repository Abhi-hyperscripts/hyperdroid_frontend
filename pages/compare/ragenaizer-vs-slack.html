Being straight

When you should stay where you are.

A comparison page that finds no reason to keep the other tool is not a comparison, it is an advert. Three honest ones.

You only need this one thing

If Slack is the only software your business runs and you have no intention of adding a CRM, projects or HR, a specialist tool will go deeper in its own lane than we will. Our argument is the stack, not the single app.

Your team is fluent in it

Retraining has a real cost, and it lands on the people who are busiest. If everyone is fast in Slack and nothing is actually breaking, the licence saving may not cover the disruption this year.

You depend on something we lack

Check the table below against the handful of things you genuinely could not work without. If one of them is missing here, that is a reason to wait — ask us and we will tell you honestly whether it is on the way.

90-Day Message History Limit

On free Slack, messages older than 90 days disappear. After 1 year, they're permanently deleted from servers.

Ragenaizer: Unlimited message history on all plans

Huddles Aren't Real Video Calls

Slack Huddles max out at 50 people, have no recording, no breakout rooms, and no host controls. Need Zoom for real meetings.

Ragenaizer: Full-featured video with recording & controls

Tool Sprawl Nightmare

Slack + Zoom + Dropbox + HR tool = 4 logins, 4 bills, 4 interfaces. Context switching kills productivity.

Ragenaizer: Everything in one platform, one login

Expensive AI Add-on
