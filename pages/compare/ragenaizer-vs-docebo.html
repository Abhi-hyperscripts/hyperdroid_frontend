Being straight

When you should stay where you are.

A comparison page that finds no reason to keep the other tool is not a comparison, it is an advert. Three honest ones.

You only need this one thing

If Docebo is the only software your business runs and you have no intention of adding a CRM, projects or HR, a specialist tool will go deeper in its own lane than we will. Our argument is the stack, not the single app.

Your team is fluent in it

Retraining has a real cost, and it lands on the people who are busiest. If everyone is fast in Docebo and nothing is actually breaking, the licence saving may not cover the disruption this year.

You depend on something we lack

Check the table below against the handful of things you genuinely could not work without. If one of them is missing here, that is a reason to wait — ask us and we will tell you honestly whether it is on the way.

What leaves your stack

The things you stop running.

Not features you gain — work you stop doing. That is usually where the real saving is, and it never appears on a licence invoice.

Quote-Based Enterprise Pricing

Docebo doesn't list prices. You book a sales call, get a multi-page quote, and discover the entry annual contract is in the tens of thousands. SMBs are priced out by design.

Ragenaizer: transparent per-user pricing, no minimums

Long Implementation Cycles

Docebo rollouts are projects with timelines and a CSM. Weeks of configuration, branding, integrations and training before your first cohort goes live.

Ragenaizer: signup → first cohort enrolled the same day

Live Sessions Need a Third-Party Tool

Docebo doesn't host video. You integrate Zoom or Webex, manage two contracts, and your learners follow links between systems for live training.

Ragenaizer: live sessions on the platform's own video

LMS Only

Docebo is an LMS. Hires, projects, video, accounting — you're integrating with a stack of separate tools. Five vendors to manage one workflow.

Ragenaizer: LMS plus the rest of the business platform
