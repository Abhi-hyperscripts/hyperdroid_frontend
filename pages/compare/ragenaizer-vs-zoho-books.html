Common Zoho Books Frustrations

Problems Ragenaizer Accounts solves out of the box

Cross-App Sync Drift

Zoho CRM and Zoho Books are "synced" but the sync is lossy. Custom fields, tags and history don't always survive. Customer records subtly drift over time and nobody knows which one is right.

Ragenaizer: one customer record, no sync needed

Five Apps to Run One Business

Books + CRM + People + Projects + Inventory. Each is a separate URL, separate UI, separate set of edge cases. New hires take weeks to figure out where everything lives.

Ragenaizer: one product covers all five

No Universal Search

Search in Zoho Books finds books records. Search in Zoho CRM finds CRM records. There's no single search bar that finds anything matching "Acme Corp" across the whole platform.

Ragenaizer: one search bar, one set of results

Fixed Assets Live in Inventory

Want to track depreciation? In Zoho-land, fixed assets are part of Zoho Inventory, a different product on a different page. The accounting team has to learn another app.

Ragenaizer: fixed assets and depreciation in the same module
