Load your item master, opening stock and party list from a spreadsheet instead of typing them in. CSV or Excel (.xlsx) — export from Marg, Tally, Busy or Excel, download a template below to see the column names, then upload. Nothing is written until you press Import — the preview shows exactly what would happen, row by row.

1 · Item master

SKU, name, barcode, category, HSN, unit, GST rate, prices — plus the description and product photographs your online shop displays.

2 · Opening stock

SKU, quantity, unit cost, batch & expiry. Import items first.

3 · Suppliers & customers

Name, GSTIN, contact and address. The GSTIN is checked against the state code — they decide IGST vs CGST+SGST on every future document for that party.

4 · Opening balances (what is owed)

What each customer still owes you, and what you still owe each supplier, as at your changeover date. Import the party list first. These are kept out of GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B — your previous system already reported those supplies.
