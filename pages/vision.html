Features

Everything your team needs to connect

From quick check-ins to company-wide presentations, Vision gives you professional meetings without the learning curve.

Crystal Clear Video

HD video that automatically adjusts to your connection. No more frozen screens or choppy audio—just smooth, professional meetings.

Share Your Screen

Present slides, demo products, or walk through documents. Viewers can zoom in on details and even capture screenshots.

Look Professional Anywhere

Working from a messy room? Use background blur or choose from preset backgrounds. AI keeps you in focus while hiding distractions.

Record & Review

Never miss important details. Record meetings automatically and access them anytime from your cloud storage.

Control Who Joins

Open meetings for quick collaboration, or use waiting rooms and invite lists for sensitive discussions. You decide who gets in.

Keep Everyone Engaged

Emoji reactions, hand raising, and live chat keep meetings interactive. See who's speaking and never talk over each other.

Live Captions (CC)

Real-time speech-to-text captions during meetings. Click the CC button and see live transcription with speaker names. Powered by Deepgram cloud STT with local Vosk/Whisper fallback. Accessible by default.

Guest Join Links

Share a link — anyone joins without an account. Perfect for client calls, interviews, and external meetings. No signup, no download, just click and join.

Meeting Types

Three meeting modes: Regular (anyone with link joins), Hosted (host must start first, lobby with admit/reject), and Participant-Controlled (allowlist-only). Choose the right security level for each meeting.

Live Captions in Real Time

Turn on captions and the AI transcribes the meeting live. Accessibility, comprehension and language support — all built in. The caption stream is captured the moment someone speaks, so nobody misses a word.

Code-Switching Transcription

Run a meeting where speakers swap between English and Hindi (or any two languages) mid-sentence? Most transcription tools choke. Vision handles it natively. Pick the meeting language, enable code-switching, and the transcript follows the conversation.

AI Meeting Assistant & Summaries

Toggle AI support per meeting. When the call ends, the AI delivers a clean summary of decisions, action items and key points — auto-generated, fully editable, or replace with your own. Stop taking notes; start being present.

Speaker Diarization & Roles

Transcripts identify who said what across the entire meeting — and you can assign roles (interviewer, candidate, client, host) to each speaker. Perfect for interviews, sales calls, focus groups and any meeting where attribution matters.

Transcript Export & Search

Every meeting transcript can be exported as text or JSON. Search across past meetings, paste sections into reports, or feed them into your own tooling. The full session history is yours.

Email Meeting Invites

Add participants and send invitation emails directly from the meeting page. The email includes the join link, meeting details and a calendar reminder. No copy-pasting links into a separate mail client.

Project Workspaces

Group meetings under a project for context — recurring stand-ups, client engagements, sprint reviews. Pull every meeting, recording and transcript for a project from one screen instead of digging through a flat list.

Integrations
