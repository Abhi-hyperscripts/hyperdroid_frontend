Why Email Here

Stop paying for what you already have

Your team already has email. What they don't have is one place to work from — where every inbox, every customer, every project and every file lives side by side. That's what this is.

Keep the email you already pay for

Gmail, Microsoft 365, your hosting provider, your custom domain — they all work. No migration, no "switch to our servers," no domain changes. Your mail keeps living where it lives. You just get a better place to work from.

Every inbox, one workspace

Connect as many mailboxes as you need. Your personal address, the shared sales@ inbox, the support queue, finance — they all show up in the same place. Switch between them or see everything in one unified view.

Your email, your infrastructure

Messages stay on the mail servers you already trust. Nothing gets copied to a third-party vendor you've never heard of. For companies in healthcare, finance, legal or government, this is the difference between a yes and a never-again.

Same tab as the rest of your business

Your team already opens CRM, Drive, projects and meetings here. Email joins them. No more eleven tabs, no more context switching, no more "where did that message go." One window, one login, everything in reach.

Replaces seats you're paying for

Every Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 user comes with an email seat you're already paying for. Consolidate onto one platform, cut the licensed seats you no longer need, and keep the mail you already have. The math usually works in the first month.

Shared inboxes, no extra licensing

sales@, support@, info@, careers@ — every team inbox your business runs. Multiple people can read and reply. Nothing falls through the cracks. No per-seat upsell, no "enterprise tier" gate.

Real-time, no refresh

Messages arrive the moment they're sent. Your inbox updates itself, your phone buzzes, your desktop gets a tap on the shoulder. No polling, no "checking every five minutes," no missing the thread that decides the deal.

Big attachments without the bloat

Send and receive large attachments — contracts, recordings, design files — without watching your storage spiral. Files stay on your mail server; we don't duplicate what's already yours.

Email that turns into action

A customer replies — promote the thread into a CRM deal. An invoice arrives — attach it to the vendor record. A client books a call — spin up a meeting. Email stops being a to-do list and becomes the front door to the work that already lives in your platform.

On-premises, your brand

Self-hosted deployment, your domain, your logo. The whole email experience can run on hardware you control, under your branding, for customers who can't hand off sensitive correspondence to anyone else's servers.

Audit-ready by default

Every send, reply, forward and delete is recorded with a full trail. Legal review, compliance audits, discovery requests — pull the answer in minutes instead of rebuilding it from screenshots and memory.

Inbox in your pocket

Works on every phone with a browser. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app — notifications, offline, the works. No App Store gauntlet, no IT rollout to install anything.

Leave whenever you want

No proprietary mailbox format, no hostage data. Your email stays on your provider; disconnect from Ragenaizer and the mail is still there, exactly where it always was. This is what "no lock-in" actually means.

Built for teams, not individuals

Every feature assumes more than one person is using the system. Shared inboxes, clear ownership, visible thread history for the people who need it. Your team shows up as a team — not a collection of disconnected inboxes.

One platform, one bill, one vendor

Email, CRM, accounts, projects, HR, meetings — all under the same subscription. One contract to negotiate, one relationship to manage, one invoice to approve. Finance and IT both breathe easier.

Most companies don't need a better email client — they need fewer apps, fewer logins, and fewer places where the next answer might be hiding. Bring email into the platform that already runs the rest of your business and the math gets easy.
