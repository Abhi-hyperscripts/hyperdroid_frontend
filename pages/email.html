Straight answers

The things people ask on the first call.

01

Do we have to change our email address?

No. You connect the accounts you already have over IMAP and SMTP, or sign in with Google. Your address, your domain and your provider stay exactly as they are.

02

Can several people work a shared address?

Yes — sales@ or accounts@ sits in the same list as personal mail, so the shared inbox is not a second window somebody has to remember to open.

03

What happens when the person leaves?

The mailbox is connected to the workspace rather than to a laptop. The customer thread stays with the business, which is the actual reason to move mail off individual clients.

04

Can we tell if a proposal was opened?

Yes, opens are tracked — so a follow-up is a decision rather than a guess. The same tracking backs the campaigns and sequences in CRM.

05

Can we schedule a send?

Yes. Compose now, send at a time you pick.

06

Does it connect to the CRM?

It is the same mail engine. Templates, campaigns and multi-step sequences in CRM send through it, so a message to a lead is on the lead rather than only in someone's sent items.

07

Is Mail billed separately?

No — it is one of the apps in the seat block, on the same login as everything else.
