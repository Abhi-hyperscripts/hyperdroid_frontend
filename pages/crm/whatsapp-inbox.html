WhatsApp Inbox

Two-way conversational WhatsApp powered by your tenant's Interakt connection. Customers reply to your WhatsApp Business number and the messages land here in real time. You can send replies from this page within Meta's 24-hour reply window without using a template.

If your tenant has multiple WhatsApp numbers, pick one in the "Sending from" dropdown — the conversation list and outbound messages are scoped to that number. Configure new numbers under Settings → Integrations → WhatsApp.
