Sequences

Multi-step cadences that auto-fire emails, WhatsApp templates, call tasks, and status flips for the leads you enroll.

What is a sequence?

A sequence is a pre-scripted, multi-day series of touchpoints that the CRM auto-fires for any lead you enroll. Think of it as a recipe you apply, and the system handles the timing.

Example: "SaaS Founder Outreach" might be Day 0 email → Day 2 call task → Day 4 WhatsApp template → Day 7 follow-up email. You enroll 50 leads on Monday — the rep doesn't have to remember anything; tasks land in their queue at the right time.

The smart bit: if the lead replies to your email, messages you on WhatsApp, or you change their status (e.g. they got qualified), the sequence automatically stops for that lead — so you don't keep hammering someone who already engaged.

What you can do here:

  • Click New Sequence to design a cadence
  • Activate or pause sequences without deleting them
  • Enroll leads from the lead detail panel or the leads list (multi-select)
  • Watch progress on each lead's timeline as steps fire
