How do I connect my Facebook Lead Ads?

Paste a System User token minted inside your own Meta Business Manager. You stay in control of the permissions, and we never touch your Facebook account directly.

  1. Open business.facebook.com. Create a Business Manager if you don't have one (free, 2 min).
  2. In Business Settings → Accounts → Pages, add the Facebook Page that runs your lead ads.
  3. In Business Settings → Users → System Users, click Add. Name it "CRM Integration", role: Employee.
  4. Assign assets to the System User — click the System User, Add Assets → Pages, pick your page, grant "Access Page" and "Manage Page". This is the step most people miss.
  5. Still on the System User screen, click Generate New Token. Select scopes: leads_retrieval, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_metadata. Set Token Expiration: Never.
  6. Copy the token (Meta only shows it once) and paste it below.

After connecting, pick which pages and forms you want, and map each form's questions to CRM fields — same as a CSV import.

How does this work?

We poll your connected Google Sheet every 2 minutes and import new rows as CRM leads. Perfect for the Meta Ads → Google Sheets → CRM flow that doesn't need Meta App Review.

  1. Click Connect Google Account and sign in. We only request read-only access to your sheets.
  2. Pick the spreadsheet and tab you want to ingest from.
  3. Map each sheet column to a CRM field (first_name, email, phone, etc.). Unmapped columns land in custom_fields.
  4. Mark the column that uniquely identifies a row (e.g. Meta's id column) so we don't duplicate leads on retry.
  5. Save — new rows start flowing into Leads within 2 minutes.

To set up Meta Lead Ads → Google Sheets, go to Meta Business Suite → Instant Forms → your form → CRM Setup → Google Sheets.

What are OAuth Apps — and do I need one?

An admin-only, one-time setup that lets your team connect Gmail or Microsoft 365 mailboxes with a single click.

  • You register your own OAuth app in Google Cloud / Azure once — you control the credentials, not us.
  • After that, every user on your tenant can sign in to their Gmail / Outlook mailbox through your app — no Google/Microsoft publisher verification required.
  • You only need this if your team uses Gmail or Microsoft 365. If everyone is on IMAP providers (GoDaddy, Hostinger, Zoho, Zoho Mail, custom SMTP), you can safely skip this section.
  • Nothing is sent yet — OAuth Apps just make the "Connect Mailbox" button work for Gmail/Outlook below.

What are Connected Mailboxes — and how do they differ from OAuth Apps?

The actual email accounts your CRM uses to send outreach and receive replies — one row per user.

  • Each user clicks Connect Mailbox and links their own address (e.g. john@company.com). Campaigns go out from that address; replies are routed back into the CRM inbox.
  • Two ways to connect:
    • OAuth (Gmail / Microsoft 365) — one-click login, requires an OAuth App above.
    • IMAP + SMTP — works for any provider (GoDaddy, Hostinger, Zoho, custom). No OAuth App needed.
  • A mailbox can be personal (only you send from it) or tenant-shared (team-wide, e.g. sales@company.com).
  • TL;DR: OAuth Apps above = admin infrastructure (set up once). Connected Mailboxes here = the per-user accounts that actually send and receive mail.
