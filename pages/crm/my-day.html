What is this page?

A daily snapshot of your work in the CRM, scoped only to you. The three sections answer three questions: what did I do (calls, emails, meetings, notes, leads worked), what came out of it (calls connected, deals won), and what still needs my attention (overdue tasks, overdue follow-ups, leads that have gone cold). Managers and admins see everyone's report on the Team Day page (coming soon).
