What is this page?

A daily snapshot of CRM work — by default scoped to you. The three sections answer three questions: what did I do (calls, emails, meetings, notes, leads worked), what came out of it (calls connected, deals won), and what still needs my attention (overdue tasks, overdue follow-ups, leads that have gone cold). If you're a manager or admin, the Rep picker (top-right) lets you switch the report target — managers see members of their teams; admins see everyone in the tenant.
