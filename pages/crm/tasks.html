What is this? — Tasks

A task is follow-up work that doesn't fit as a call, an email or a note — send the signed contract, chase a document, prepare a quote. Each has a priority, an optional due date, and someone it's assigned to.

My Tasks is what's on your plate. Overdue is anything past its due date that nobody has finished. All Tasks is the whole team's list, filterable by status.

Tip: the Sales Assistant can create tasks for you — try "remind me to send the Meridian quote on Friday". They show up here.
