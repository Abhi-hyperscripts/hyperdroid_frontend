Storage

Ragenaizer stores your files in object storage that you own and control. Pick your provider and paste an access key — we do the rest, including creating the bucket. Works with Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2, DigitalOcean Spaces, Backblaze B2, Wasabi or your own MinIO. Your files never leave your account, and you can revoke our access at any time from your provider.

All you need

  1. An account with any S3-compatible provider.
  2. An access key with read, write, delete and create-bucket.

We create the bucket, work out the endpoint and pick the addressing style. If your key cannot create buckets, make one yourself and name it under “Use a bucket I already have”.

What we can reach

  • Only the ragenaizer/ folder, never anything else in the bucket.
  • The endpoint must be public HTTPS. Private and internal addresses are refused.
  • Your secret is encrypted before storage and never shown again.
