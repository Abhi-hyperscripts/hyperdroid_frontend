Storage

Ragenaizer stores your files in object storage that you own and control. Connect a bucket from any S3-compatible provider — Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2, DigitalOcean Spaces, Backblaze B2, or your own MinIO. Your files never leave it, and you can revoke our access at any time from your provider.
