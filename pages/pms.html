Features

Everything you need to manage projects and bill clients

From client onboarding to timesheet approval — a complete project management system built for teams that track time and bill accurately.

Client & Project Hub

Client management with contacts, industry, addresses. Nest projects under clients. Sub-project support for complex deliverables.

Task Boards

Todo/In Progress/In Review/Done workflow. Priority levels, assignees, due dates, and time estimates for every task.

Time Tracking

Daily time entries with hours + minutes. Optional task linkage. Billable/non-billable flagging for accurate invoicing.

Weekly Timesheets

Submit and approve workflow. Bulk time entry for the week. Manager approval with comments. Weekly summaries per project.

Billing & Rates

Per-member billing rates on projects. Auto-calculated billable amounts. Time summaries with cost breakdowns per client and project.

Activity Feed

Full audit trail. Track who changed what, when. Filter by project, member, or action type. Never lose context on any change.

Engineering-Grade Issue Tracker

A complete bug & issue tracker built right in. Project codes auto-generate human-readable issue keys like RAG-47 you can paste into Slack or git commits. Filter by status, severity, priority, component, assignee, reporter, type, resolution or keyword search.

Severity, Priority & Reproducibility

Real bug-report fields, not toy ones. Severity (critical / high / medium / low / trivial), priority (P1–P4), reproducibility (always / sometimes / rarely / unable), plus environment, component, steps to reproduce, expected and actual results. Triage like a real engineering team.

Status Workflow with Resolution Codes

Reported → Confirmed → In Progress → QA Testing → Resolved → Closed → Reopened. Close issues with a real resolution: fixed, won't fix, duplicate, cannot reproduce, by design. First-response and resolved-at timestamps are captured automatically so you can measure your real SLA.

Threaded Comments & Attachments

Drop screenshots, log files or repro videos right onto an issue or onto an individual comment. Full threaded replies, edit and delete with audit. The system also creates a system comment every time the status changes — your timeline tells the full story.

Watchers, History & Bulk Update

Subscribe to issues you care about and get notified on every change. Every field change is captured with the actor, timestamp and old/new values — no more "who closed this?" Mark duplicates to auto-close and link to the original. Bulk-update status, priority or assignee across many issues at once.

Kanban Board & My Issues View

Drag issues across status columns on the kanban board for any project. Switch to "my issues" to see everything assigned to you or reported by you. Get statistics for any project — counts by status, severity, priority, component, plus average time to resolve.

Auto-Created Project Meeting Rooms

Every project gets its own video meeting room with one click — no scheduling, no link sharing, no fishing for the right room name. Run stand-ups, client calls and retros without leaving PMS.

Invoice Straight from a Project

When a milestone is hit, raise an invoice in Accounts in two clicks. The customer record is shared with finance, the project is the source of truth for hours and rates, and the invoice posts to the ledger automatically.
