Instant Messages

Messages arrive the moment you hit send. No refreshing, no waiting—just natural, flowing conversations with your team.

Chat During Meetings

Share links and notes while on a video call. Everything stays in one place, so you can review discussions even after the meeting ends.

Drop Files Easily

Drag and drop documents, images, or any file right into the conversation. They're automatically saved to your Drive for later access.

Nothing Gets Lost

Scroll back through your conversation history anytime. Find that link someone shared last week or revisit important decisions.

Stay in the Loop

Get notified when someone joins, leaves, or when important things happen. You'll always know what's going on with your team.

Include Everyone

Guests can join meeting chats too—no account required. Perfect for client calls or interviews where not everyone is on your team.

Real-Time with SignalR

Messages arrive instantly via SignalR WebSocket connections. No polling, no delays. Typing indicators, online presence, and read receipts — all in real-time.

Cross-Service Messaging

Chat is woven into every Ragenaizer product. Start a conversation from a meeting, share a Drive file, discuss an HRMS leave request, or coordinate on a PMS task — without switching apps.

Files Auto-Save to Drive

Every file shared in chat automatically saves to Drive. No manual downloads, no lost attachments. Full audit trail and access control on every shared file.

Direct & Group Conversations

Start a 1:1 chat with anyone or spin up a group conversation with your team, your clients or your interview panel. Add members in bulk, assign admins, remove people when projects end. Each conversation has its own roster and history.

Edit & Delete Messages

Made a typo? Edit it. Sent the wrong file? Delete the message. Every change is auditable so nobody can rewrite history without leaving a trace, but everyday corrections feel like the apps you already use.

Read Receipts & Unread Counts

See exactly who's read your message in every conversation. Per-conversation unread counts mean you never lose a thread, and your team always knows what they've actually seen.

Online Status & Presence

Set yourself to online, away, busy or offline. Your team sees real presence in real time. Heads-down on a deep work block? Set yourself to busy and skip the interruptions.

Archive Conversations

Project's done? Archive the conversation — it stays searchable but doesn't clutter your sidebar. Bring it back any time. Soft-delete is also there for the ones you really want to clear out.
