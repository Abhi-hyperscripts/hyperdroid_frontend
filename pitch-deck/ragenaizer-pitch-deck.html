10 — Why Now

The post‑SaaS consolidation wave.

For fifteen years the market wanted "best in class" point tools. That era is ending. Three forces are pushing every service business toward consolidation — at the same time.

01 · SaaS Sprawl Peaked

87 SaaS apps per mid‑market company. Procurement, IT, security and finance are all signaling the same thing: no more vendors.

02 · AI Fragments Further

Twelve tools = twelve AI bills. Every SaaS vendor is launching its own AI add‑on at +$20/user/month — stacked on top of the base price.

03 · Cost Pressure Mounting

SaaS is the first line item CFOs cut. Funding tightened, margins matter again, and "what can we consolidate?" is the loudest question in every boardroom.

04 · Data Sovereignty Rising

Self‑hosted is now a board‑level ask. GDPR, India DPDP and regional cloud mandates have pushed sovereignty from fringe to default for regulated buyers.

05 · Integration Fatigue

The glue layer is exhausted. Zapier, Make, MuleSoft — buyers are tired of paying twice: once for the tools, once for the integrations between them.

06 · The Window

Incumbents are too heavy. Newcomers are too narrow. Zoho/Odoo carry legacy weight; Notion/Linear stop at the team. The gap for an AI‑native Business OS is open right now.
